Privacy Policy

GUVELI Publisher · Operated by GUVELI Studio · Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Who we are

GUVELI Publisher (the “App”) is a Mac application operated by GUVELI Studio (“we”, “us”) that lets creators schedule and publish their own videos and photo posts to their own TikTok accounts. Privacy contact: gvl777medicina@gmail.com.

2. Data we access from TikTok

Only after you log in with TikTok and approve access, and only for the account you connect:

  • user.info.basic: your open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected and where content will be posted.
  • video.publish: to publish the videos and photo posts you choose to your account with the settings you choose, and to read the publishing status of those posts.
  • video.upload: to send content you choose to your TikTok drafts/inbox so you can finish it in the TikTok app.
  • Creator settings TikTok returns before posting (allowed privacy levels, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are enabled, maximum video length), so each post respects them.

We do not access your followers, messages, contacts, analytics, other users' data or any data about viewers.

3. How we use it

Only to show the connected account, to publish the content you selected with the settings you selected, and to show you whether each post was published. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or training of any model, and we do not sell it.

4. Where data is stored

The App runs on your own Mac. Access and refresh tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on that Mac; they are never written to plain files, never printed in logs and never sent to any server other than TikTok's. Your videos and captions stay on your Mac until you publish them. For photo posts, TikTok requires images to be fetched from a verified web address: the App places the selected images temporarily on this website (gvl777medicina-ctr.github.io/media/…) only so TikTok can download them, and removes them after publishing.

5. Sharing

We share data only with TikTok, as needed to publish your content. We do not sell, rent or share data with any other third party.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while your account stays connected. You can disconnect at any time with “Disconnect” in the App, which revokes access and deletes the tokens, or in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). To request deletion of any data, write to gvl777medicina@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and must not be used by anyone under 18.

8. Cookies

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above.
